Moustaqim: Islamic Resources

Moustaqim.com is a multilingual Islamic resources platform: full Quran reader, prayer times for 14,000+ cities, a database of 2,400+ verified Muslim names, a hadith encyclopedia and an Arabic dictionary. Free in 32 languages.
